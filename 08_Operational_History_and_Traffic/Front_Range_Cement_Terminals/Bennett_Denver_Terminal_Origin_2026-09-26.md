# CEMEX Denver Terminal (3405 Rail Park Dr, Rocky Mountain Rail Park): how far back the paper trail goes

Research date: Sat Sep 26, 2026. All times are Mountain Time (MT). Built from public records (Adams County Clerk & Recorder, Adams County Accela, CDPHE, company and trade materials). Press releases, LinkedIn posts, job postings and other items marked as such are secondary sources; the recorded documents and county permit records are the primary ones.

## Bottom line
- **Earliest verified CEMEX-specific date: October 21, 2024.** That is the Effective Date of the "Absolute Net, Unsubordinated Ground Lease" between Rail Land Company, LLC (landlord) and CEMEX Construction Materials South, LLC (tenant). The date is stated in the recorded Memorandum of Lease (Adams County Reception No. 2025000069262) and in two recorded SNDAs (2025000069327 and 2025000069328).
- **Earliest verified public mention: October 22, 2024.** Rocky Mountain Industrials (RMI) issued a press release announcing a "long-term commercial land lease and rail services agreement" with an unnamed "multi-billion dollar global construction materials company." The PDF's file name and its embedded document title both contain "CEMEX" (created 10/22/2024 at 2:11 PM MDT). The Wayback Machine captured the PDF on Nov 6, 2024.
- **Earliest possible (inferred) evidence: on or before July 12, 2024.** A metro-district valuation study dated July 12, 2024 says the developer had received Letters of Intent for "Lots 1, 1A, 5, 8 and 9." CEMEX's site is Lot 1A (6.08 acres) according to Adams County permit USE2025-00029. The LOI holder is not named, so linking it to CEMEX is an inference.
- **Jurisdiction:** the site is in **unincorporated Adams County**, not the Town of Bennett. Adams County is the zoning authority (P-U-D), and "Bennett, CO 80102" is only the mailing address. The Oct 22 - Dec 11, 2025 building and temp-meter permits listed below are **Adams County** permits in the county's Accela system (ADAMSCO).
- Before August 2025, CEMEX's activity is contractual: the lease signed in October 2024, two follow-on agreements in Oct and Dec 2024, and contractor licensing in Dec 2024 to May 2025. The lease **Initial Term commenced August 5, 2025**. No public record was found of CEMEX construction or site work at the site before August 2025.

## Gaps and what couldn't be accessed
- The Ground Lease itself, both amendments, and the Agreement Regarding Dates are **not recorded**; only the memorandum and SNDAs are public. Terms such as rent, contingencies, and the feasibility period are unknown. The Agreement Regarding Dates probably governs the period from Oct 2024 to Aug 2025 (inferred).
- Accela result paging did not work under automation (ASP.NET postbacks). Broad searches showed only the top 10 of the stated totals (for example "Rocky Mountain Rail Park" = 75 results, "RMRP" = 37). Manual check: search https://aca-prod.accela.com/ADAMSCO/ for "Rocky Mountain Rail Park," sort by date ascending, and review 2024–mid-2025 records. Also open the "Processing Status" tab on USE2025-00029 for workflow dates. Plan documents sit behind ProjectDox, which returned an error.
- LOI holder for Lot 1A in the July 2024 study is unnamed.
- CDPHE online systems were not queried. Earlier air or stormwater pre-filings could be checked there, or via CORA, but CORA was out of scope.
- Nothing found from Xcel, the metro district, or tap applications specific to CEMEX before August 2025.
- No evidence of physical CEMEX site work before August 2025. RMI drone videos from 2025 show other lots and the rail infrastructure.
