# NorCo_Evans_Terminal

Public records on the Mountain Cement Company (Eagle Materials) "Northern Colorado Distribution Facility" / "NorCo Terminal." It is a rail-to-truck dry cement terminal at 23273 47th Ave (Weld County Road 33), LaSalle mailing address, in the City of Evans, Weld County. Parcels: 105703401001 (Lot 1) and 105703401002 (Lot 2), Mountain Cement Company Minor Subdivision (Weld rec. 5043502).
