### Is it capacity-constrained? (ESTIMATE)
- **Permit tonnage: probably not binding today.** CDPHE records show about 218,800 t unloaded Jan–Sep 2023 (see `Front_Range_Cement_Terminals_2026-09-26.md`). Annualized, that is about 270–290k t/yr (estimate, discounting the seasonally weaker Q4). That is roughly half the 600k unloading cap and about 55–60% of the **500k truck-loading cap, which is the binding permit limit**. So Denver has about 200k+ tpy of on-paper headroom. I did not find throughput figures for 2024–2026; the TAC reports use emission factors and don't state tonnage.
- **Practical constraints are the bigger issue:**
  - The COC's cease-operations-on-visible-emissions requirement limits reliability.
  - Four silos give limited storage.
  - The dense urban site has no room to expand, and the land-use record shows no expansion filings.
- **Inference:** Eagle is adding capacity in the Front Range through **NorCo rather than Denver**. Denver is "ready" for Laramie volume only up to its existing headroom and under COC scrutiny.
- **What would change this:** a new APEN or permit-modification application for 031-0178 in CDPHE OnBase, a Denver commercial building permit or SDP for new silos.

### Estimated operational date: NorCo (ESTIMATE)
- **Range: Dec 2026 – Apr 2027. Most likely month: February 2027.** Confidence: low-to-medium.
- **Evidence for "soon":**
  - Under construction per the Aug 18, 2026 staff report.
  - The last legal prerequisite for a CO (the Development Agreement) was cleared Aug 18, 2026.
  - ROW ordinance adopted Sep 1, 2026.
  - An operator was being hired in July 2026 (companies usually hire 3–6 months before start).
  - The original corporate plan tied the terminal to a 2H 2026 startup.
- **Evidence for "not before winter":**
  - The site still says "coming soon."
  - Off-site access improvements (paving and turning radii on 47th Ave) must be completed before operation, and road work slows in winter.
  - The escrow/bond is due about Oct 2, 2026.
  - A 135-ft silo/dome structure plus new UP trackage typically takes 9–15 months (general industry experience). Construction start is undocumented, and the "under construction" statement dates only to Aug 2026.
  - No CDPHE air authorization is visible yet.
- **Logic of timing:** Eagle has every incentive to open NorCo before the new kiln's tons arrive (commissioning early 2027) and before the spring 2027 construction season. It could start by moving cement from the existing Laramie lines or Eagle's network.
- **Downside case:** if the air permit or access road work slips, the terminal opens around Apr–May 2027, together with the start of Laramie new-line output.

## 4. When does Laramie expansion volume actually reach the Front Range? (ESTIMATE)
- **Mechanics:**
  - Commissioning starts early CY2027 (per the Jul 29, 2026 call).
  - Typical new kiln line: first clinker about 1–3 months after commissioning begins, then a ramp to rated output over about 3–9 months (general industry experience, not an Eagle statement).
  - Cement ships by rail from Laramie to Denver (existing) and NorCo (new).
- **Estimate:**
  - **First new-line cement shipped: about Mar–May 2027.**
  - **Meaningful incremental Front Range tons: about Jun–Sep 2027 (most likely ~June 2027),** aligned with the peak 2027 construction season.
  - **Full ~1.2M tpy run-rate: late CY2027 to early CY2028** (FY2028 2H).
  - Management's "coming online over the next year or so" (Jul 29, 2026) is consistent with this.
- Confidence: medium-low. Risks are further commissioning slippage (one quarter has already slipped), old-kiln reliability during the overlap (the Q1 FY27 failure cost about $6M), and NorCo readiness.
