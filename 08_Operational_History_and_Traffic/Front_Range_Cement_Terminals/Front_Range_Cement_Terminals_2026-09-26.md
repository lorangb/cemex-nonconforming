## Add-on: Commerce City vs. Bennett, and inbound vs. outbound rail at Lyons
- **Commerce City terminal:** it existed. It opened 9/21/2017 with 5,000 st of silos and was fed by BNSF from Lyons. **Whether it still operates is not verifiable from public air records**, because it is not in CDPHE OnBase, probably APEN-exempt. Third-party directories still list "CEMEX Denver Cement Terminal, 8850 Yosemite St, Henderson 80640".
- **Does Bennett replace it?** Probably, but that is an inference. Bennett carries the same "Denver Terminal" name. It is far bigger: 4 silos and a 790,746 t/yr truck-haul basis versus a 5,000-st silo site. Nothing public states that Commerce City will close.
  - Bennett is on **Union Pacific** (via Patriot Rail's Front Range Railroad), while Lyons and Commerce City are on **BNSF**. So Bennett is not a simple single-line extension of Lyons rail shipments. It points to cement sourced over UP from outside Colorado (or imported).
- **Lyons rail direction:**
  - **Outbound:** 2017 trade press says the Commerce City terminal was "serviced by an existing rail line out of the company's Lyons cement plant". This implies Lyons ships finished cement out by BNSF rail, likely from the P013 cement silos/load-out. The Title V permit does not name a railcar load-out separately, and no public tonnage for outbound rail was found.
  - **Inbound:** P050 imported cement: about 14,450 t in 2006–07, then 0 until the 5/22/2026 startup (see `05_Air_Quality_Permit_95OPBO082/P050_Rail_Unloader_Analysis/`). Raw-material rail receipt at P014 is coal and iron only (CEMEX to CDPHE, 2/21/2023).
