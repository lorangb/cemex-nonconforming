# Front_Range_Cement_Terminals

Notes and public records on the cement storage and distribution terminals that serve Colorado's Front Range.

- `Front_Range_Cement_Terminals_2026-09-26.md`: inventory of Front Range cement terminals.
- `Bennett_Denver_Terminal_Origin_2026-09-26.md`: the CEMEX Bennett/Denver terminal (AIRS 001-2353).
- `Eagle_Mountain_Cement_Colorado_Terminal_Timing_2026-09-26.md`: Mountain Cement (Eagle Materials) Denver terminal (AIRS 031-0178) and the Northern Colorado terminal as of Sep 26, 2026. The NorCo material is updated in `NorCo_Evans_Terminal/`.
- **`NorCo_Evans_Terminal/`**: public records on the Mountain Cement Northern Colorado ("NorCo") terminal at 23273 47th Ave / WCR 33, City of Evans. Includes:
  - the Aug 18, 2026 Development Agreement, Ord. 869-26 and staff reports;
  - Council minute excerpts;
  - Weld County Assessor photos (silos erected by Jan 14, 2026) and 2027-roll sketches (four 135-ft silos plus a 2,121-sf building);
  - the CDPHE/EPA record of construction-stormwater certification COR425765 (effective Nov 5, 2024).
  See its README.
