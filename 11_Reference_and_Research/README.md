# 11 — Reference & Research

Background and supporting material that bears on CEMEX's operation in the area or the
non-compliance claims.

- **Cement_Kiln_Dust/** — references on cement kiln dust (CKD).
- **Emissions_and_Health/** — emissions and public-health materials (e.g., mercury, kiln
  environmental-justice analyses).
- **Comparison_Plants/** — other cement operations used for comparison, including plants that
  import/truck limestone (relevant to the "must mine locally" question).
- **CEMEX_Corporate/** — CEMEX corporate and greenhouse-gas materials.
- **Historical/** — the Colorado Geological Survey publication, mineral-activity reports, and a
  1998 Southdown newsletter.
- **SOSVV/** — Save Our St. Vrain Valley materials on the environmental and health impacts of
  cement manufacturing.

> General cement-industry **decarbonization** research was set aside under `_Not_Relevant/`.

- **Comparison_Plants/Penalties_vs_US_Cement_Portfolio/** — Lyons fines and penalties compared with
  CEMEX's other US cement plants, 2020–2026 (analysis; revenue figures are estimates).

- **Legal_Reference_Unsafe_Structures/** — public court opinions on county and city unsafe-building, dangerous-building and demolition orders. They cover county authority, due process, takings and abatement-cost liens.
  - *Hibbard v. County of Adams*, 900 P.2d 1254 (Colo. App. 1994), and *County of Adams v. Hibbard*, 918 P.2d 212 (Colo. 1996)
  - *Gold Vein LLC v. City of Cripple Creek*, 973 P.2d 1286 (Colo. App. 1999)
  - *State Dep't of Health v. The Mill*, 887 P.2d 993 (Colo. 1994)
  - *Bruce v. Ogden City*, No. 22-4114 (10th Cir. Dec. 1, 2023) (unpublished)
  - Opinion text as published by Justia, or the court's own PDF; the source URL is at the top of each file.
  - Not included: *Bruce v. Pikes Peak Regional Building Dept.*, No. 20CA0531 (Colo. App. Oct. 21, 2021) (not published under C.A.R. 35(e)). A copy from an official source could not be retrieved.
  - Related fire-district and county records are in `14_Fire_Protection_and_Building_Safety/`.
