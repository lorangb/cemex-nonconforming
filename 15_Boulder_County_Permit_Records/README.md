# 15 – Boulder County Permit Records (CEMEX Lyons plant area)

These are public Boulder County Community Planning & Permitting records tied to the CEMEX Lyons cement plant (5134 Ute Hwy) and related CEMEX, Southdown and Southwestern Portland parcels. They come from the County's Accela portal and were retrieved in October 2026. Files are grouped by decade, then by record number.

- **`RECORD_INDEX.csv` / `RECORD_INDEX.md`** list all 137 records returned by the County search. Each entry gives the date, type, County description, Accela link and what was done with it here:
  - **posted:** County files are mirrored in this folder.
  - **linked (Accela):** large modern construction or plan sets (2013 onward: telecom, flood, emissions equipment, grading), plus the 2022 Dowe Flats extension docket and the 2015 Dowe Flats review. These are available at the Accela link and are not mirrored, to keep the repo small.
  - **no file available online:** the County lists the record but publishes no file.
  - **not posted:** residential or private-individual permits, weed or zoning enforcement complaints, and the 2026 appeal (AP-26-0001). Within posted records, files that are neighbor or public comments, mailing lists, or individuals' emails are also withheld.
- **BP-05-0129 and BP-08-0871** (rail unloader dust collector) are in `05_Air_Quality_Permit_95OPBO082/P050_Rail_Unloader_Analysis/Boulder_County_Building_Permits/`.

All items are **documented** (County records). The quotes below are taken from the files.

## Selected records (documented)

**1965–1967: plant construction and zoning**
- **BP-65-8040 (1965):** Rocky Mountain Cement Company, "Modern cement plant: initial phase of site preparation and primary foundations," zone "Industrial." *Microfiche card, frame 5.*
- **BP-67-10137 (1967):** Martin Marietta Corporation, "Modern Cement Plant," valuation 15 million dollars, zone "GI-General Industrial." *Microfiche card, frame 4.*
- **Z-67-0360 (application filed June 27, 1967; BOCC resolution July 24, 1967):** the application covers the S½ SW¼ of Section 21, T3N R70W (with exceptions), and lists the acreage as "60 acres" (handwritten). It asks to change the zoning from "A, Agricultural" to "GI, General Industrial." The resolution says the property it had been rezoned to "I-Industrial" on February 25, 1965 (affirmed in *Orth v. Board of County Commissioners*), then erroneously shown as "A-Agricultural" on later final maps. The board orders the maps corrected. *Pages 1–3.*

**1973–1990: kiln, coal and clinker work**
- BP-73-18153: coal storage silo (1973).
- BP-79-0463: combustion change/kiln (1979).
- BP-84-0182: foundation for clinker cooler (1984).

**1993: Dowe Flats application (SU-93-14a)**
- Southdown, Inc. (Southwestern Portland Cement) states the purpose: "Ensure long-term stability of the cement plant. Given historical levels of permitted output of cement products, it has been estimated that the proposed mine plan would provide cement plant reserves for 25 years." *PDF page 5.*
- "Existing industrial facilities at the cement plant are maintained." *PDF page 67.*
- "The existing cement plant has been a neighborhood feature for 24 years." *PDF page 73.*

**1994: access permit**
- LS-94-0097: Southwestern Portland Cement Co. survey plat, 70 acres. No file is online.
- TAP-3143 (Dec. 22, 1994): access permit for an office trailer, applicant Southwestern Portland Cement Co.

**2022: Martin Marietta appeal (AP-22-0003)**
- The Martin Marietta memorandum attaches the Western Mobile Lyons Comprehensive Plan (1990s). Page 3-12 (PDF page 60, Bates REMAND000040) says:
  - "the Southdown's cement plant and mining area dominates the landscape in this area of the St. Vrain River Valley";
  - "The Burlington Northern provides rail service to Western Mobile's existing mining operation as well as to Southdown and Ideal's cement terminal located west of Highway 36."

**2023–2026: termination case and rezoning**
- ZON-23-0003: the County's April 10, 2024 Notice of Termination, CEMEX's responses and exhibits, the traffic studies, and the May 19, 2026 Rescission. The County's independent traffic review (Nov. 6, 2025) states that "by 2019, the rail line was no longer exporting material from the CEMEX plant." *Page 4.*
- Z-23-0002 / Resolution 2024-030 (2024): Lyons-area rezoning to Agricultural.
