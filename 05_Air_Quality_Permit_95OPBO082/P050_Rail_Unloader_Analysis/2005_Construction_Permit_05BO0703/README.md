Key text in the permit (page 1): equipment is "One (1) Custom, cement railcar unloading and handling system ... One (1) hopper to receive cement from bottom unloading railcars ... One (1) pneumatic transfer system for conveying cement to silo/s." The facility is described as a "Portland cement manufacturing facility." Condition 1 says the permit expires if the owner "does not commence construction/modification or operation of this source within 18 months." Condition 8 (page 2): "Unloading and handling of cement shall not exceed 50,000 tons per year." Condition 3 requires compliance self-certification "within one hundred and eighty days (180) after commencement of operation." Condition 6 requires an operating and maintenance plan that "shall commence at startup."
