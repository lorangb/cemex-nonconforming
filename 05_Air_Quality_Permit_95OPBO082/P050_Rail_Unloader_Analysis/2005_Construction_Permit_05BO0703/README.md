## Key permit text

**Page 1**
- Equipment: "One (1) Custom, cement railcar unloading and handling system ... One (1) hopper to receive cement from bottom unloading railcars ... One (1) screw conveyor ... One (1) pneumatic transfer system for conveying cement to silo/s ... one (1) pulse jet baghouse, identified as BH 725-4."
- Facility: "Portland cement manufacturing facility."
- Condition 1: the permit expires if the owner "does not commence construction/modification or operation of this source within 18 months."

**Page 2**
- Condition 3: compliance must be shown "within one hundred and eighty days (180) after commencement of operation."
- Condition 6: the O&M plan "shall commence at startup."
- Condition 8: "Unloading and handling of cement shall not exceed 50,000 tons per year."

**Page 3**
- Condition 10: emission limits are "as calculated in the Division's preliminary analysis."

The permit contains no reference to prior rail unloading or to replacing existing equipment.
