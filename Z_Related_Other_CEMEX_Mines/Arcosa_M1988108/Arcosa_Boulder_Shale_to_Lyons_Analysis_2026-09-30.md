# Arcosa Boulder (Jefferson County) shale and the CEMEX Lyons plant: public-record research

Compiled 2026-09-30 from public records only. Legend: **[V]** verified in a primary document; **[I]** inference; **[NP]** not public / not found. Sections 1-4 were drawn from the public documents named in each line; items marked "(not re-checked)" were carried from working notes.
Companion data: `MSHA_0504415_Arcosa_Boulder_quarterly_hours_employment.csv` (this folder). Related: `Z_Related_Other_CEMEX_Mines/Wyoming_Mine_PT0658/Lyons_Raw_Material_Sourcing_Weaver_Limestone_and_Shale_2026-09-30.md`.

## 1. Facility identity [V]
- **Arcosa Lightweight - Boulder**: rotary-kiln expanded-shale plant and Pierre Shale quarry, **11728 Hwy 93, Boulder, CO 80303**. It is physically in **Jefferson County** (north of Rocky Flats, ~11 mi N of Golden); "Boulder" is the mailing address. Operator page: https://arcosalightweight.com/about/production-facilities/boulder-co/
- **MSHA mine ID 0504415** "Arcosa Boulder Plant #1604"; operator ARCOSA LWB, LLC (0134299); controller Arcosa, Inc (0144281); SIC 1459 common shale. MSHA Mines.txt (pulled 9/30/2026): status **Intermittent as of 07/22/2026**, 10 employees. (Third-party scraper sites showing "Active" are stale.)
- **DRMS permit M-1988-108** "Western Aggregates", permittee Arcosa LWB, LLC; 112c construction regular operation; Jefferson County; 214.21 permitted acres; 94.0 acres affected; bond $6,019,868 (SI-2, 2/18/2025). Amendments AM-001 (2/1993), AM-002 (7/1994), AM-03 (filed 2019, approved 3/17 and 4/7/2020; see `2020-02-28_REVISION - M1988108.pdf` in this folder). Operator succession TXI Operations LP to TRNLWB/Arcosa LWB approved 10/7/2013. DRMS electronic file base URL: https://dnrweblink.state.co.us/drms/ElectronicFile.aspx?docid=<ID>&dbid=0&repo=DRMS
- **CDPHE APCD**: AIRS 059/0409; Title V **95OPJE084** (first issued 1/1/2004; renewed 5/1/2026, expires 5/1/2031); construction permit 88JE372-x. EPA ICIS-Air CO0000000805900409 (major source).
- Ownership chain (MSHA ControllerOperatorHistory): Western Aggregates/Inco 1989-97, then Texas Industries (TXI) 11/25/1997 (TXI Operations LP from 3/2005), then Trinity Industries / ARCOSA LWB 3/23/2013 (TXI-Trinity asset exchange, SEC 8-K ex. 99.1: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/97472/000117184313001120/exh_991.htm), then Arcosa, Inc. after the November 2018 spin-off.
- Identity cautions: a search-engine claim that the plant "was Lafarge/Holcim" is **unsupported** (no Lafarge link in the MSHA history). EPA ECHO lists the same address under former names Bluestone Aggregate Co and TXI Operations dba Western Aggregates. Arcosa's **Bethany, Indiana** lightweight plant was idled in early 2026 (WISH-TV, 3/13/2026), which is a different plant. Another Jefferson County shale pit, Church Harvey Mine (MSHA 0500340, CMT Excavating, Hwy 93 near Golden), exists; it is **not verified** as a CEMEX supplier.

## 2. Supply arrangement / contract status
**Status: UNKNOWN. The contract itself is [NP].**
Evidence that a relationship exists [V]:
- DRMS inspection of CEMEX permit M-1977-208, signed 3/2/2023 (site visit 2/13/2023): "Shale material is being sourced from the Arcosa mine in Jefferson County." Repeated in the inspection signed 3/21/2025 (site visit 3/2025). (`03_State_Mining_Permit_M1977208/`)
- CEMEX's 9/23/2024 moisture table to CDPHE lists "Arcosa Shale" as a separate feed (Title V renewal INFO_TO_SUPP, `05_Air_Quality_Permit_95OPBO082/`).
- Stantec traffic study 8/28/2023 (Boulder County exhibit): inbound shale trucks come from the west via northbound US 36 / eastbound SH 66 (per CEMEX staff); limestone-plus-shale truck entries 132/day on 6/15/2023 versus an average of 17/day in June 2022. The Dowe Flats quarry closed 9/30/2022 (County denied an extension 2-1 on 9/29/2022).
Unverified [advocacy source]: complaint ZON-23-0003 (https://www.townoflyons.com/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Item/11211?fileID=22834) alleges 10+ 35-ton trucks per hour (~80+/day) through Boulder on 28th St / Hwy 36.
The `CEMEX Historical Data.xlsx` workbook in `08_Operational_History_and_Traffic/Historical_Data/` (sheet "Inbound Material Modes") assumes 350,000 tpy of Arcosa shale over 35 miles from 10/1/2022. That is an **unsourced assumption**, not a record.
No public termination, expiry or renewal notice was found in SEC filings, DRMS, CDPHE, Boulder County or news. Arcosa's SEC filings reviewed (10-K 2/27/2026, 10-Qs 5/1 and 8/5/2026, FY2024 10-K, DEFM14A, 8-Ks 4/1 and 9/4/2026) do **not** mention CEMEX, Boulder or a Colorado lightweight plant.

## 5. Takeaways
- [V] Arcosa Boulder plant idle since 1/12/2026; MSHA "Intermittent" as of 7/22/2026; 10 employees.
- [I] Quarry hours down ~64% (Q1) and ~86% (Q2) year over year suggest raw shale shipments (to CEMEX or anyone) were greatly reduced or stopped by Q2 2026, unless quarry hours do not reflect stockpile loadout.
- [NP] Contract terms, tonnage, and whether CEMEX switched shale source.
- Whether CEMEX still needs raw materials cannot be settled from Arcosa data. CEMEX imports nearly all of its raw material; the Weaver limestone totals show need through February 2026. CEMEX's MSHA Q3 2026 hours are due about 10/15/2026.
