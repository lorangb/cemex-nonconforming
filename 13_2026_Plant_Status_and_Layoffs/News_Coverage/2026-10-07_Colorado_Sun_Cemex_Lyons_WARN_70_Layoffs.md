# Colorado Sun, Oct 7, 2026: "Cemex at Lyons warns of 70 layoffs, more than half of staff at controversial cement kiln"

- **Outlet:** The Colorado Sun, by Michael Booth, published 3:10 PM MDT, Oct 7, 2026.
- **Link:** https://coloradosun.com/2026/10/07/cemex-layoffs-lyons-boulder-county/
- **Syndicated / follow-on:** Rocky Daily News (10/7), https://rockydailynews.com/cemex-laying-off-big-portion-of-lyons-cement-factory/ ; The Slope (10/8, a rewrite of the Sun story that adds that CDLE hasn't yet posted the notice on its public WARN spreadsheet), https://theslope.co/news/cemex-cuts-70-jobs-at-controversial-lyons-cement-plant

**Status: Reported (news article).** What the article itself reports (summarized, not reproduced):
- CEMEX issued a WARN notice warning it will lay off 70 workers beginning in November, which the article calls more than half its total staff.
- **Boulder County confirmation:** according to county spokesperson Gloria Handyside, CEMEX filed a WARN Act notice with the Boulder County Attorney's office on Sept. 22. The notice said layoffs would begin Nov. 21 and involve "at least 70 employees," and it was signed by a company vice president.
- **CEMEX's response:** CEMEX spokesperson Randy Stuart gave the same "rebalancing staffing levels" statement and said a WARN notice had been issued. The company said sales and distribution will continue and that it isn't closing all "operations." Asked a follow-up about the kiln, Stuart said only: "The cement operation at Lyons is not ceasing."
- **Headcount figures cited:** a recent MSHA filing (112 people, as cited in the article), CEMEX's "more than 100," and a 2023 CEMEX flyer (90).
