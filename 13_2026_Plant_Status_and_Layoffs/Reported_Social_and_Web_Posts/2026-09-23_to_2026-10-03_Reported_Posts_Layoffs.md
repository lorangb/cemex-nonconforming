# Reported posts about CEMEX Lyons layoffs, Sept 23 to Oct 3, 2026

**Status: Reported, not independently verified.** These are public social-media and website posts. They aren't CEMEX records, and this archive doesn't vouch for them. The documented record is CEMEX's 9/22/2026 letter to employees, which says layoffs commence 11/21/2026, and its 9/23/2026 statement to The Colorado Sun, which says "cement sales and distribution at the Lyons plant will continue." Both are in `../CEMEX_Public_Statements/`.
