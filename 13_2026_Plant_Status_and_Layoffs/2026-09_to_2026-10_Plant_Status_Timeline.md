# CEMEX Lyons plant status: September-October 2026 factual timeline

Compiled 2026-10-03 (MT) from public records and the repository's own documents. Each line gives its source and its status:
- **Documented**: a primary or official record was read.
- **Reported, not independently verified**: stated in a secondary report or working note; no primary record of the event itself is in this archive.
- **Inference**: reasoning from documented facts, labeled as such.

This note records what the documents say. It draws no conclusion about whether the plant will close or about the legal effect of any entry.

## E. Caveats
- Section A rows were read in the original records or page images; employee names and contact details appearing in those DRMS documents are not reproduced here.
- The meaning of the 9/3/2026 "last activity" entry is not established by the form; it is not evidence of any operational change.
- The 9/22/2026 WARN notice (at least 70 employees, first layoff 11/21/2026) is the first CEMEX record giving a layoff count. It does not state that production will stop.
- Nothing in the public record reviewed shows a temporary cessation of mining, a planned plant closure, or a change to any permit status as of 10/1/2026.
