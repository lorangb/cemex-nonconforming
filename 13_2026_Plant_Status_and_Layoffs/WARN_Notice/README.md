# CEMEX WARN Act notice, September 22, 2026 (Documented)

`2026-09-22_CEMEX_WARN_Act_Notice_to_CDLE_and_Boulder_BOCC_70_Employees_2026-11-21.pdf` is a scan of the copy CEMEX sent to the Boulder County Board of County Commissioners. Page 1 is the envelope (CEMEX, 10100 Katy Freeway Suite 300, Houston; USPS Certified Mail); page 2 is the letter. The scan was made 10/7/2026.

What the letter says:
- **Dated** September 22, 2026, from Cemex Construction Materials South, LLC, signed by a CEMEX vice president.
- **Addressed to** the State of Colorado Dislocated Worker Unit (633 17th St., 7th Floor, Denver; RRTeam@state.co.us) and the Boulder County Board of County Commissioners (1750 33rd St., Boulder).
- **Re:** "WARN Act Notification," given "pursuant to the Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification ('WARN') Act of 1988."
- CEMEX "is conducting layoffs as part of a staff rebalancing at the Lyons cement plant located at 5134 Ute Hwy, Longmont, CO 80503."
- "The expected date of the first layoff is November 21, 2026 and this action is expected to affect **at least 70 employees**."
- "Additional information pursuant to 20 C.F.R. § 639.7(e) is available upon request."

It doesn't mention the kiln, production, closure, or a terminal.

**Redaction:** the CEMEX contact person's name, phone number and email address are blacked out. Nothing else is altered.

**Context (Documented):** MSHA's latest quarterly filing (2026 Q2) lists 113 employees at the Lyons plant, so at least 70 is about 62% or more of that headcount. Through 10/1/2026, the CDLE public WARN list showed no CEMEX entry (see `../Public_Records_Monitoring_Log/`). As of 10/8/2026 it shows one: received 10/7/26, 70 permanent layoffs, reason "Staff Rebalancing at Lyons Cement Plant". The sheet's begin date (11/2/26) differs from the letter's 11/21/2026. The letter CDLE links to has the same text as this one (linked from https://cdle.colorado.gov/employers/layoff-separations/layoff-warn-list) (Documented).
